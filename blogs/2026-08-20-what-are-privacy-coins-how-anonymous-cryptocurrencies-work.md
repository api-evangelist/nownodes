A privacy coin is a cryptocurrency built to hide the sender, receiver, and amount of every transaction, using cryptography instead of a company’s privacy policy. Bitcoin and Ethereum publish that same data forever on a ledger anyone can read; Monero, Zcash, and Dash are engineered specifically so they can’t.
